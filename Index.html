About Bloom & Bliss

Bloom & Bliss was founded by Mrs. Adams with a passion for bringing joy through flowers.

Our story started in Durban with a small shop and a big dream: to combine warm hospitality with beautiful floral design. Today we serve weddings, events, and daily deliveries across KZN.

Our Mission

To create lasting memories with personalized service and artistic flower arrangements.
